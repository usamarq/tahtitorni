From Workshop A of the 14th International UbiComp Summer School (UBISS 2026) at
the University of Oulu: how little sensing and computation does coordinated
multi-robot behaviour actually need?

I built a leader-follower system of three robots in which the two followers
used only ultrasonic sensors to lock onto and track the leader, and implemented
the control logic in CircuitPython on a physical robot platform. The system was
completed and presented at the workshop.

The linked repository holds my study notes from the workshop, not the full
build.
