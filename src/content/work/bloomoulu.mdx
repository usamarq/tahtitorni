A team project from GrowthHack 2026: a digital platform for the University of
Oulu Botanical Garden, built with Team Meraki.

The platform is multilingual (English, Finnish, Swedish) and covers a
plant-adoption program, QR-linked plant pages, an interactive map, and a lobby
kiosk display. An AI chat guide answers visitors' questions from the garden's
own plant data and shows its sources.

My part: I built and deployed the React front end, including the interactive
maps and live weather, and the AI chat guide. The interface is designed to meet
WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standards.

The full platform, front end and back end, is live. It has no custom domain
yet, so its address carries the server's IP. A front-end-only preview runs
separately, and my commits are visible in the team repository.
