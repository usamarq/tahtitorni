A Deep Learning course project at the University of Oulu: detecting diabetic
retinopathy from retinal images, computer vision applied to healthcare
diagnostics.

I developed a deep learning model for the task, experimenting with different
neural network architectures and optimization techniques. The repository walks
through the comparisons, including Grad-CAM visualizations, attention
mechanisms and ensembles.

Rather than training from scratch, I used transfer learning on pretrained
convolutional networks including ResNet and VGG, adjusting the model weights
for the task, and compared them against each other.
