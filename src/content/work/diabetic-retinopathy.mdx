A Deep Learning course project at the University of Oulu: detecting diabetic
retinopathy from retinal images in the DeepDRiD dataset, computer vision
applied to healthcare diagnostics. It was an individual project, and I
completed all of it on my own, working through the tasks the course set.

Rather than training from scratch, I used transfer learning on pretrained
convolutional networks including ResNet and VGG, adjusting the model weights
for the task, and compared them against each other while experimenting with
different architectures and optimization techniques.

Around the models I built, in PyTorch: image augmentation (CutOut, CLAHE and
custom transformations), spatial, channel and self-attention modules, single-
and dual-image model variants, and max-voting and weighted-average ensembles.

To see what the networks were looking at, I implemented Grad-CAM heatmaps in
PyTorch, showing which regions of a retinal image the models relied on for
their predictions. The repository walks through all of it.
