A Big Data Processing course project at the University of Oulu, on a
97,405-game Steam dataset.

I analyzed the dataset with Apache Spark and PySpark: exploratory analysis
followed by modeling with Random Forest and Linear Regression, identifying
success factors such as engagement, recency, and indie/action genres. The
Random Forest Regressor reached 69% variance explanation.
